# HR Performance & Employee Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview
The HR Performance & Employee Analytics Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into employee status, department distribution, work-life balance, gender distribution, employee satisfaction, engagement, performance, and training costs.

The objective is to help understand workforce trends and support data-driven HR decisions.

## Dashboard Preview
![HR Analytics Dashboard](screenshots/hr_dashboard.png)

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Employees: 3K
- Active Employees: 2K
- Terminated Employees: 387
- Average Satisfaction: 3.03
- Average Engagement: 2.94
- Average Performance: 2.07

## Key Dashboard Visualizations
1. Employee Status Distribution – Active vs Terminated employees.
2. Work-Life Balance Analysis – Employee count by work-life balance score and employee status.
3. Department-wise Performance and Satisfaction – Comparison across departments.
4. Department-wise Employee Distribution – Employee count across departments.
5. Gender Distribution – Female and Male employee distribution.
6. Average Training Cost by Department – Training cost comparison across departments.

## Tools & Technologies

## Project Objectives
- Analyze employee distribution and employment status.
- Understand employee satisfaction and engagement.
- Compare performance across departments.
- Explore work-life balance patterns.
- Analyze training costs across departments.
- Present HR data through an interactive dashboard.
